# NNNN. Short title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, the alternatives that
were on the table and why the decision had to be made at all. Two short
paragraphs at most. State facts, not intentions.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, followed by the concrete shape it
takes in the repository. Always cite the files that embody the decision, so a
reader can check the ADR against reality:

- `path/to/file.ext` — what it does for this decision.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what a future maintainer has to
keep doing because of this. Include the bad parts; an ADR that only lists
upsides is marketing.
